## Key Learnings by Module

### Module 1 - Intro to Financial Statement Analysis (5 errors)

- **FSA uses reports prepared by companies** (not analysts)
- **Annual report is NOT a SEC filing** - the 10-K is the SEC filing
- **Proxy statement** = executive compensation disclosure
- **MD&A** = full capital resources discussion (including off-balance-sheet items)
- **SOX requirement**: auditor must opine on internal controls

---

### Module 2 - Income Statements (19 errors - mostly EPS)

#### Discontinued Operations
- Plan to dispose = report **immediately** as discontinued (don't wait for completion)

#### EPS Rules (Critical)
- **If-converted preferred stock**: DON'T subtract preferred dividends from numerator
- **If-converted bonds**: ADD BACK after-tax interest to numerator + add conversion shares to denominator
- **Treasury stock method**: Net shares = options exercised - shares repurchased at average market price
- **Antidilutive test**: If diluted EPS > basic EPS, EXCLUDE the security entirely
- **Stock splits**: retroactive adjustment to beginning of year for all periods shown

#### Classification
- Fire/disaster = **continuing operations** (not discontinued, not OCI)
- Unusual items (post-2015 US GAAP) = within continuing operations
- **IFRS income definition**: asset enhancements/liability decreases (excludes owner contributions)

#### Revenue Recognition & Accruals
- Deferred income = cash received before delivery (**liability**)
- Prepaid expense = cash paid before consuming (**asset**)
- Policy change = retrospective restatement to periods shown in report (not all prior periods ever)

---

### Module 3 - Balance Sheets (9 errors)

#### Investment Securities Classification
- **US GAAP**: ALL equity securities go to FVTPL (through income). OCI election only available under IFRS.
- **Trading securities**: unrealized G/L through P&L, flows to retained earnings
- **AFS securities**: unrealized G/L through OCI, accumulates in AOCI
- **HTM securities**: unrealized G/L NOT recognized (carried at amortized cost)

#### Other Key Rules
- **Goodwill** = Purchase price - Fair value of net assets (NOT book value)
- **Common-size balance sheet** reveals leverage changes (not efficiency)
- **Near-term obligations** = liquidity assessment = current ratio (not financial leverage)

---

### Module 4 - Cash Flows I (7 errors)

#### Classification Rules (US GAAP vs IFRS)

| Item | US GAAP | IFRS |
|------|---------|------|
| Interest paid | ALWAYS operating | Choice: operating OR financing |
| Dividends paid | ALWAYS financing | Choice: operating OR financing |
| Interest received | ALWAYS operating | Choice: operating OR investing |
| Dividends received | ALWAYS operating | Choice: operating OR investing |

#### Indirect Method Adjustments
- **Cash paid for OpEx**: subtract BOTH decrease in prepaids AND increase in accrued liabilities
- **Revenue to cash from customers**: Revenue = Cash from customers + increase in AR
- **COGS to cash paid**: COGS = Purchases - increase in Inventory

#### Framework Identification
- Interest/dividends received classified as **INVESTING** = IFRS only (identifies the framework)

---

### Module 5 - Cash Flows II (7 errors)

#### FCFF Calculations
- **FCFF from CFO (interest in financing section)**: NO interest add-back needed (already excluded)
- **FCFF from CFO (interest in operating section)**: ADD BACK Int x (1-t)

#### Analysis Rules
- **Common-size CF base** = % of revenue or % of total inflows/outflows (NOT net cash flow)
- **Cash-to-income ratio** = CFO / Operating income
- **First step in CF analysis** = identify major sources and uses (big picture first)
- **Don't add depreciation to CFO** - it's already included when starting from net income

---

### Module 6 - Inventories (24 errors)

#### FIFO vs LIFO (Rising Prices)
- **FIFO ending inventory** = newest costs = closest to replacement value
- **LIFO inventory** = oldest costs = least likely to need write-down
- **FIFO COGS** uses OLD costs = below replacement cost
- **LIFO COGS** approximates replacement cost
- **LIFO appears more EFFICIENT** (higher turnover from lower inventory)
- **FIFO appears more PROFITABLE and LIQUID** (higher NI, higher current ratio)

#### LIFO-to-FIFO Conversion
- FIFO Inventory = LIFO Inventory + LIFO Reserve
- FIFO COGS = LIFO COGS - Increase in Reserve

#### Write-Downs
- Write-down improves ONLY activity ratios (turnover)
- Write-down HURTS profitability, liquidity, and solvency ratios
- **IFRS**: write-down reversal allowed (limited to original cost)
- **US GAAP**: NO reversal permitted

#### Valuation Rules
- Post-2016 US GAAP: non-LIFO methods use NRV (same as IFRS)
- Only LIFO still uses "market value" (replacement cost with ceiling/floor)
- **Declining prices**: REVERSE all FIFO/LIFO effects described above

### Module 7 - Long-Term Assets (13 errors)

#### Impairment
- **IFRS**: Recoverable amount = MAX(FV - costs to sell, Value in use)
- Impairment effects: lower net profit margin, higher D/E, higher turnover, NO effect on cash

#### Useful Life & Amortization
- **Renewable at minimal cost** = indefinite life = NO amortization
- **Remaining useful life** = Net PP&E / Depreciation expense (not gross PP&E)
- **Interest coverage** should use ALL interest (capitalized + expensed)

#### Revaluation & Fair Value
- **Investment property (fair value model)**: gain goes to Income Statement
- **PPE (revaluation model)**: gain goes to OCI (unless reversing prior loss)
- Revaluation resets carrying value; subsequent depreciation based on NEW revalued amount
- Revaluation up effects: lower ROE, lower ROA, lower D/E, lower leverage, NO effect on margin

#### Other Rules
- Always use **AVERAGE** balances for turnover ratios
- US GAAP disclosure requirements are less exhaustive than IFRS
- Internally developed intangibles: higher operating cash outflows, lower total assets

---

### Module 8 - LT Liabilities & Equity (11 errors)

#### Leases (Post-2019)
- **ALL leases >1 year** go on balance sheet (both finance and operating)
- **Operating lease (US GAAP lessee)**: single "lease expense" line (no separate depreciation/interest)
- **IFRS lessees**: ALL leases treated as finance (capitalize PV, expense = depreciation + interest)
- **Lessor - operating**: keeps asset on books
- **Lessor - finance**: derecognizes asset

#### Pensions
- **Pension OCI items** = actuarial gains/losses ONLY
- **Pension P&L items** = service costs + net interest expense

#### Share-Based Compensation
- Stock options: BOTH risk-averse AND risk-seeking behavior are drawbacks
- Share-based comp objective = attract employees + align with **SHAREHOLDERS** (not management)
- Annual compensation expense = total option value / vesting period

---

### Module 9 - Income Taxes (4 errors)

- **Higher tax rate acquirer** pays MORE for tax loss carryforwards (worth more to them)
- **DTL condition**: tax expense > taxes payable (OR asset carrying value > tax base)
- **DTA condition**: asset carrying value < tax base (more deduction available later)
- **DTL balance calculation** = (Carrying value - Tax base) x Tax rate at that date (not cumulative difference)

---

### Module 12 - FS Modeling (11 errors)

#### Valuation & Analysis
- **Porter's five forces**: assess margins vs BOTH history AND competitors
- **Pre-tax metrics** for cross-country comparison (operating margin, not ROIC)
- **Terminal value with multiples**: adverse outlook = lower multiple applied
- **Base rate neglect** = ignoring reference class data (behavioral bias)

#### Modeling Mechanics
- **Working capital from company ratios** = bottom-up approach
- **Research costs** sit below gross profit (don't affect gross margin)
- **Growth capex** = net of sale proceeds
- **Effective interest rate method** links Income Statement to Balance Sheet (reflects debt changes over time)
